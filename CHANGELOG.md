# Changelog

The user-facing changes in each App Store release, newest first. Each section is also that
release's "What's New" text.

## 1.1 — 2026-10-10

Every image, not just the cover. Click a record's cover to see its back cover, labels and inner
sleeves.

Folders, your way. Move a copy to another folder from its page or its context menu, or drag it onto
a folder in the sidebar.

Your copy at a glance. A record's page now shows its folder, the date you added it and your own
fields, such as condition and notes.

Plus faster record pages and many small fixes.

## 1.0 — 2026-09-30

First release on the App Store, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
