# Privacy Policy

**Catalogista** — effective 27 September 2026

Catalogista does not collect any personal data. It has no server of its own: it talks only to
Discogs, on your behalf, with the token you give it.

- **Your token stays on your device.** Catalogista stores your Discogs Personal Access Token in
  the Keychain and sends it only to Discogs, to read and change your own collection.
- **Your collection stays on your device.** Catalogista keeps a copy of your collection and its
  cover art on this device so you can browse it offline. Disconnecting your account deletes that
  copy.
- **Only Discogs sees your requests.** Catalogista connects to `api.discogs.com` and the Discogs
  image server. Discogs handles that data under its own
  [privacy policy](https://support.discogs.com/hc/articles/360009334513).
- **No analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.** The app contains no third-party code.

This application uses Discogs’ API but is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Discogs.
‘Discogs’ is a trademark of Zink Media, LLC.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/lysyi3m/catalogista/issues).
